Roofing CRM vs General CRM

Why roofing teams outgrow generic CRMs.

General CRMs start at the lead

A general CRM assumes you already have a lead and a contact. Roofing sales usually start earlier — on the map, at the property, at the door. That gap is where a general CRM makes roofing teams improvise.

Roofing CRMs start at the property

A roofing CRM brings property intelligence and canvassing into the system, so leads are created from real properties and carry their context forward into inspections, quotes, and jobs.

Materials are part of the job

General CRMs rarely touch purchasing. Roofing runs on materials, so connecting supplier pricing and ordering to the job — as RoofSpan does with ABC Supply — removes a whole layer of duplicate work.

Field reality

Roofing happens outside, often with poor signal. A roofing CRM's field app has to keep working offline and sync later; a bolt-on mobile view usually doesn't.
