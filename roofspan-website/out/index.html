FAQ

Common questions

What do I need to run RoofSpan Office?

RoofSpan Office installs and runs on your company's own Windows computer and opens through a local browser interface. There is no separate hosted web application to log into.

How does RoofSpan Mobile connect?

Field users install the free RoofSpan Mobile app and pair it with your company's RoofSpan Office installation to securely connect while away from the office.

How much does RoofSpan cost?

RoofSpan is $49 per user per month with a five-user minimum, so pricing starts at $245 per month.

Where does my data live?

Your operational roofing database lives with your RoofSpan Office installation on your company's own Windows system.
